# AI & SaaS Writing Samples

Two long-form articles on the real costs of AI — electricity, water, and what happens to your thinking when you outsource it. Written in plain English, sourced from the IEA, Gartner, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, and peer-reviewed research (MIT Media Lab, Gerlich 2025, Microsoft Research / CMU).

## Articles

1. **Your AI Habit Has Three Bills: Power, Water, and Your Brain** — [ai-three-bills.md](ai-three-bills.md)
   The full picture: data centers heading for ~945 TWh by 2030, 17.4 billion gallons of water a year, and the cognitive debt of letting a chatbot do your synthesis.

2. **The Thinking Bill: What Happens When You Stop Doing the Hard Part** — [ai-thinking-bill.md](ai-thinking-bill.md)
   A deep dive on cognitive offloading: the MIT EEG study, the 58% vs 69% retention gap, and how to use AI as a bicycle instead of a wheelchair.

All facts verified against named sources, 2026-11-09. No fee amounts are stated (none are relevant to this topic).
